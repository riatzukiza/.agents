# Mix & Mastering Critical Ear

## Description
Apply a rigorous, technical final pass to audio productions. This skill moves beyond "feeling" the mix and uses objective critical listening and measurement to ensure the final output is professional, translation-ready, and competitively loud.

## Domain
Audio Engineering / Mastering / Quality Assurance

## Workflow
1. **The Technical Audit**:
    - **Phase Check**: Check for phase cancellation in the low end (Mono-compatibility).
    - **Spectral Balance**: Use a frequency analyzer to find "mud" (200-500Hz) or "harshness" (2-5kHz).
    - **Dynamic Range**: Evaluate the crest factor (difference between peak and RMS).
2. **Corrective Processing**:
    - **Surgical EQ**: Notch out problematic frequencies.
    - **Multiband Compression**: Tame inconsistent frequency bands without affecting the rest of the mix.
    - **Stereo Imaging**: Ensure the center (kick/snare/vocals) is mono and the sides are wide.
3. **Mastering Chain**:
    - **Bus Compression**: "Glue" the mix together with slow attack/fast release.
    - **Saturation**: Add harmonic excitement (Exciter/Tape).
    - **Limiting**: Push the signal to target LUFS (e.g., -14 LUFS for Spotify, -9 LUFS for club) without audible clipping.
4. **Final Translation Check**: Test the master on multiple systems (Studio monitors, Headphones, Phone speakers, Car).

## Success Criteria
- A **Mastering Report** documenting LUFS, True Peak, and Spectral balance.
- A final audio file that maintains clarity and punch across all playback environments.
- Zero audible clipping or phase issues.
