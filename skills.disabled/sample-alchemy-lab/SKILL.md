# Sample Alchemy Lab

## Description
A specialized workflow for transforming "garbage" audio (field recordings, noise, raw transients) into high-fidelity, professional-grade sample packs. This is the process of anamorphosis: turning the unrecognizable into the usable.

## Domain
Sound Design / Sample Production / Experimental Audio

## Workflow
1. **Source Ingestion**: Collect raw audio sources (field recordings, accidental noises, voice fragments).
2. **Deconstruction**:
    - **Slicing**: Break raw audio into microscopic transients.
    - **Time-Stretching**: Extreme slow-down or speed-up to reveal hidden textures.
    - **Pitch-Shifting**: Finding the "musical" center of a non-musical sound.
3. **Alchemy (Processing)**:
    - **Granular Synthesis**: Using grain clouds to transform a single click into a lush pad.
    - **Heavy Saturation**: Applying tube/tape distortion to add weight and harmonic richness.
    - **Spectral Filtering**: Carving out resonant frequencies to create "synthetic" tones from organic sources.
4. **Curation & Normalization**:
    - **Naming**: Apply a consistent naming convention (e.g., `SBA_Kick_Industrial_01.wav`).
    - **Gain Staging**: Normalize samples to a consistent peak level.
    - **Trimming**: Ensure zero-crossing start/end points to avoid clicks.

## Success Criteria
- A curated **Sample Library** of unique, characterful sounds.
- Evidence of "source transformation" (where the original sound is unrecognizable but the result is musical).
- High-fidelity exports ready for use in a DAW.
